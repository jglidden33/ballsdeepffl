How Scoring Works

Point Values

  • Championship won4 pts
  • Championship game appearance1 pt
  • Playoff appearance1 pt
  • 10+ win season2 pts
  • Survivor Tourney winner1 pt
  • Season points leader1 pt

Entry Criteria

  • League member, active or former—
  • Lifetime score of at least25 pts
Scores are calculated retroactively from the 2012 inaugural season using data that's already verified elsewhere in the workbook — Championship Game Appearances count title-game losses too, not just wins. Survivor Tourney Winner is the one manually-tracked category. If a manager leaves the league, their lifetime score stays on the books; it does not transfer to a replacement.
