How It's Built

  • 14-week regular season. Every manager plays all 11 other managers exactly once across weeks 1–11, then plays the other three managers in their seed group a second time across weeks 12–14.
  • Seed groups come from the 2025 final standings: 1–4, 5–8, and 9–12.
  • Week 1 is set by seed: #1 vs #2, #3 vs #4, #5 vs #6, #7 vs #8, #9 vs #10, #11 vs #12.
  • Week 14, the final week, is always a rematch against one of the three managers in your group of four.
  • No rematch happens within 4 weeks of the first meeting — every group-of-four second matchup is scheduled at least four weeks after the first.
